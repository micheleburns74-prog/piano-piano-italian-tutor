One word, five small steps

Each word is a short guided lesson, about five minutes. You never have to hold more than one thing in your head.

Hear it and read it

A clear Italian voice, then the word split into syllables with the stressed one marked, so you know where to lean.

Say it and record it

Record yourself, play it back next to the model, and decide for yourself how close it feels. Nothing is scored.

Stuck? Slow down

"I'm having trouble" breaks the word into one syllable at a time with a simple mouth cue, then puts it back together.

  • 59 everyday phrases in 12 short sets: greetings, introductions, directions, food, offices and documents, numbers.
  • Starter memory tricks for every phrase, a sound-alike or a picture. Write your own any time.
  • Sound drills for tricky sounds such as the single R, double letters and "gli".
  • Gentle review: older phrases come back on a spaced schedule, a few at a time.

Comfortable to use, on your terms

Larger text, wider letter and line spacing, high contrast, tinted page colors, dark mode, and a one-button mode for tired days. A timer that suggests a rest, and a short session option for low-energy days.

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. Your progress is saved in your own browser. Your recordings are kept in memory in the open tab and are never uploaded. The one exception is the optional pronunciation check, which is off until you turn it on: while it listens, Chrome sends your voice to its speech service (Google) to work out what you said.

No account, no tracking. Piano Piano has no sign-up, no analytics and no ads. The only outside requests are for the page fonts from Google Fonts and, if you turn it on, the pronunciation check.

You are in control. You can save a backup file of your progress in Settings. Clearing your browser data removes your progress, so keep a backup if it matters to you.
